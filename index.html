Radar-only orbit determination

Thesis work · initial orbit determination through precise orbit determination

A tracking radar returns range and range-rate, but no angles. That missing line-of-sight direction leaves a single tracklet structurally underdetermined — four unknowns against two equations — so the usual angles-only machinery does not transfer.

I built an end-to-end MATLAB pipeline that takes raw tracklets from three ground sites and returns a refined orbit with element uncertainties. It runs initial orbit determination through a multi-revolution Lambert solve, then batch weighted least squares with EGM2008 gravity, NRLMSISE-00 drag, and third-body perturbations.

The core contribution is a square three-tracklet formulation: enforcing velocity continuity at the middle epoch closes the direction problem without gridding the solution space. I characterized measurement noise three independent ways and traced fit quality to a single geometric discriminant — the minimum cross-site Lambert transfer angle — which explains why near-antipodal baselines fail and which pass combinations to trust.

Electric propulsion hardware

Fabrication, integration, and test

I fabricate porous tungsten electrospray emitters and carbon nanotube field emission neutralizers, and I have run Hall thruster test campaigns end to end: chamber preparation, integration, diagnostics, and teardown.

The reason I keep one foot in the lab is that it changes how I write software. Knowing what a measurement costs to produce — and how it fails — is what makes an estimator honest about its own error.
